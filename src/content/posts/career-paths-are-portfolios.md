The ladder metaphor suggests one direction and a fixed sequence. Real technology careers branch, converge, pause, and sometimes deliberately move sideways.

## Name the assets you are building

- **Craft:** the ability to make reliable things.
- **Judgment:** choosing what should be made.
- **Context:** understanding a domain deeply.
- **Relationships:** earning trust and helping others succeed.

Choose a six-month experiment with a learning goal: lead a project, deepen a specialty, work closer to customers, or teach what you know.
